    About this map

    Floods are Greece's deadliest weather hazard. This map shows where they struck, who they killed and what they cost.

    Major floods (1887–2025) come from the HANZE database of European floods (Paprotny et al.). It lists only significant floods with recorded deaths, damage or people affected. Each one is drawn in the middle of the areas it hit, not at the exact town.

    Local flood reports (2012–2018) are village-level records collected by the Greek Ministry of Environment & Energy for the national flood-risk plans.

    Satellite flood maps show land seen under water by the EU's Copernicus Emergency Management Service.

    Official flood zones are the Ministry's flood hazard and risk maps under the EU Floods Directive, plus Copernicus European flood models.

    Read with care

    • Older floods are under-recorded, so the rise in recorded floods since 2000 partly reflects better reporting, not only more floods.
    • Damage is known for only 25 of the 121 major floods. Deaths are deaths in Greece only (e.g. not Storm Daniel's victims in Libya).
    • Each major flood is drawn in the middle of the areas it hit, not at the exact town. A flood that hit two areas counts in both.
    • Village reports (2012–2018) depend on how each region reported. HANZE v3.0.2 is a beta release.
